Excel Tip #15 for Accountants 📊
Sales growth % — this month vs last month — in one formula!

D2 (growth %): =IFERROR(C2/B2-1,"-")

1️⃣ Last month's sales in Column B, this month's in Column C
2️⃣ Formula in D2, drag down, then format Column D as %
3️⃣ A minus value means sales dropped ✅

Example: last month ₹1,00,000, this month ₹1,20,000 → 20% growth. IFERROR shows "-" when last month is zero (a new customer).
