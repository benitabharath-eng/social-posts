AI News 📉 1 in 5 company AI projects gave no value (yet)!

A new study by Anthropic looked at almost 300 AI projects in 58 US companies. 55% raised output or quality, but 19% have delivered no business value so far. A common blocker: AI made more output than people had time to review.

How to make AI pay off in your office:
1️⃣ Start with one clear task
2️⃣ Plan who checks the AI output
3️⃣ Measure the time saved every month ✅
