Excel Tip #8 for Accountants 📊
Split GST from an inclusive amount — one formula!

C2 (taxable value): =ROUND(A2/(1+B2),2)

1️⃣ Total (incl. GST) in Column A, GST rate in Column B (type 18%)
2️⃣ GST amount in D2: =A2-C2
3️⃣ CGST = SGST = D2/2 (for IGST, use D2 as it is) ✅

Example: ₹1,180 at 18% → taxable ₹1,000, GST ₹180 (CGST ₹90 + SGST ₹90).
