Excel Tip #19 for Accountants 📊
Formulas that copy themselves into every new row — press Ctrl+T!

Click any cell in your data → Ctrl+T → OK

1️⃣ Click inside your data and press Ctrl+T (tick "My table has headers")
2️⃣ Type a formula once in a new column — it fills the whole column
3️⃣ Add new rows at the bottom: they get the formula, filters and format automatically ✅

Bonus: Pivots and charts built on a Table grow with it — just Refresh.
