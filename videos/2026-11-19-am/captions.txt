Excel Tip #12 for Accountants 📊
Make overdue bills turn RED automatically!

Conditional Formatting rule: =$C2<TODAY()

1️⃣ Select your bill rows (for example A2:E500), with due date in Column C
2️⃣ Home → Conditional Formatting → New Rule
3️⃣ "Use a formula…", paste the rule, choose a red fill → OK ✅

The $ before C keeps every cell looking at the due date column, so the whole row turns red. Tomorrow, new overdue bills turn red by themselves.
