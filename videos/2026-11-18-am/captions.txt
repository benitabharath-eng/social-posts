Excel Tip #11 for Accountants 📊
Month-wise sales report in one formula!

C2 (month name): =TEXT(A2,"mmm-yy")

1️⃣ Bill date in Column A, amount in Column B
2️⃣ Formula in C2, drag down (15-11-2026 → Nov-26)
3️⃣ Insert Pivot: month in Rows, amount in Values = month-wise total ✅

Tip: if the Pivot sorts months A–Z (Apr, Aug, Dec…), use =EOMONTH(A2,0) instead and format the column as mmm-yy — months stay in date order.
