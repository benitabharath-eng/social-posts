AI News 🏪 OpenAI gives free AI help to small businesses!

OpenAI started a pilot called OpenWorks (Nov 24). It pays local people to help small business owners start using AI, and gives owners three months of ChatGPT Business for free, so they can test if it is worth it.

How to try AI in your business:
1️⃣ Start with one boring task (bills, replies, reports)
2️⃣ Count the hours it saves each week
3️⃣ Keep customer data out of AI ✅
