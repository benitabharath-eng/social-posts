AI News 📊 Excel's AI can now help with reconciliations!

Microsoft is rolling out new accounting features in Copilot in Excel: reconcile accounts, draft journal entries and explain variances. Worldwide rollout started on Nov 13 and should finish by late November.

What accountants should know:
1️⃣ Needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence
2️⃣ AI prepares the work, you review it
3️⃣ Never post an entry without checking ✅
