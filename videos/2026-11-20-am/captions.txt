Excel Tip #13 for Accountants 📊
Find the LAST purchase rate of any item instantly!

B2 (last rate): =XLOOKUP(A2,P!B:B,P!D:D,"-",0,-1)

1️⃣ Purchase register in a sheet named "P", in date order: item name in B, rate in D
2️⃣ Item name in A2 of your report
3️⃣ Formula in B2, drag down ✅

The last argument -1 makes XLOOKUP search from the bottom, so you get the most recent rate, not the first one. "-" shows when the item is not found. XLOOKUP needs Excel 365 / 2021.
