AI at Office · Sunday Idea 💡
Let AI write your payment reminder mail in 1 minute!

Copy this prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude:
"Write a short, polite but firm payment reminder for invoice [No], amount [Rs], due [date]."

1️⃣ Keep the [brackets] — don't type real customer details into AI
2️⃣ Paste the draft into your own mail and fill in the real details there
3️⃣ Read it once, then send ✅

Bonus: add "Make it firmer — this is the second reminder" for overdue customers.
