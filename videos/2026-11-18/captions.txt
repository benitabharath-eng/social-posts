AI News 🚀 Word, Excel & PowerPoint now inside Copilot!

At Microsoft Ignite (Nov 17), Microsoft made "Office in Copilot" generally available, so you can work on Word, Excel and PowerPoint files from inside the Copilot app. It also showed Autopilot, an always-on AI agent that keeps working for you (early-access program for now).

What it means for you:
1️⃣ One app for chat + documents
2️⃣ Mainly for company Microsoft 365 users
3️⃣ Learn Excel basics — you need them to check what AI does ✅
