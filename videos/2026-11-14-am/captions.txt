Excel Tip #7 for Accountants 📊
Catch duplicate invoice entries instantly — before they reach your GST return!

=IF(COUNTIF($A$2:A2,A2)>1,"Dup","")

1️⃣ Invoice numbers in Column A
2️⃣ Formula in B2, drag down
3️⃣ Filter "Dup" and check those rows ✅

The first entry stays blank; every repeat after it is marked "Dup". The $A$2 lock makes the range grow as you drag.
