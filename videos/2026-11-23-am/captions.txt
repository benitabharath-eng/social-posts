Excel Tip #16 for Accountants 📊
Split names in 1 second — no formula — with Ctrl+E (Flash Fill)!

Column A: Sri Balaji Traders - Madurai
Type in B2: Sri Balaji Traders

1️⃣ Type one example of what you want in B2
2️⃣ Click B3 and press Ctrl+E
3️⃣ Excel fills the whole column by itself ✅

Works for city names, first names, GSTIN parts, invoice numbers and more. Flash Fill gives plain values, so run it again if Column A changes.
