AI News 👀 Your phone's AI can read your screen!

New AI features from Google and Apple can look at what is on your phone or computer screen. The Signal messaging app has now started blocking this AI screen reading and limiting screenshots, so its chats stay private (Nov 22).

Protect your work data:
1️⃣ Check which apps your phone's AI can see (Settings)
2️⃣ Turn it off for bank and office apps
3️⃣ Close payroll and salary files when you are not using them ✅
