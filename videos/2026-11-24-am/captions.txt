Excel Tip #17 for Accountants 📊
You drag a formula down and the answers go wrong? Press F4!

GST rate in E1, amount in Column B → C2: =B2*$E$1

1️⃣ Type =B2*E1 in C2
2️⃣ Click on E1 inside the formula and press F4 (it becomes $E$1)
3️⃣ Now drag down — every row uses E1 ✅

Why: without $, Excel moves E1 to E2, E3… as you drag (those cells are empty, so you get 0). The $ locks the cell. Laptop? Try Fn+F4.
