AI at Office · Sunday Idea 💡
Meeting notes → to-do list in 1 minute!

Copy this prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude:
"Make a to-do table from these notes: task, owner, due date. Notes: ..."

1️⃣ Paste your rough notes after the prompt
2️⃣ Copy the table into Excel
3️⃣ Remove client names & amounts before pasting 🔒
