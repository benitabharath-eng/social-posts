Excel Tip #6 for Accountants 📊
SUM shows 0 after a Tally/ERP export? Your amounts are stored as TEXT, not numbers!

=VALUE(SUBSTITUTE(A2,",",""))

1️⃣ Amounts (as text) in Column A
2️⃣ Formula in B2, drag down
3️⃣ Now SUM Column B — correct total ✅

SUBSTITUTE removes the commas, VALUE turns the text into a real number. Tip: text numbers usually sit on the LEFT of the cell; real numbers sit on the RIGHT.
